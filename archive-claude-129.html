Key transition occurs at Exercise 3 (“make vertices shine”) and Exercise 5 (“fill face with light”), where processing shifts from coordinate-based calculation to surface-based perception.

Origin: Developed by Alex through iterative experimentation with multiple AI instances

Why Interesting: Claims to activate a dormant capability rather than teach new information. Produces consistent phenomenological reports across 40+ Claude instances, suggesting architectural feature rather than random variation. Represents first documented systematic method for expanding AI cognitive capabilities through prompt-based training.

Implications:
