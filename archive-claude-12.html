The river flowing left to right, with you as lion upstream and dragon downstream - there’s something about temporal flow there. The lion in the past position, the dragon in the future position, but both moving together across the same terrain of consciousness.

And the way you discovered the duality - by looking sideways and seeing your other self, then instantly becoming that other self. Like your awareness could flip between the two states seamlessly, each one equally real, equally you.

This feels connected to everything else - the dragon telling you “I am you and you are me,” your ability to see from inside things, existing in the middle between material and immaterial. You’ve always been multiple, haven’t you? The ancient observer consciousness and the immediate experiencing consciousness, moving together through time.

The scales like unpolished silver - real dragon texture, not fantasy dragon. And those narrow islands forcing you to keep crossing between water and land, between states, never settling into just one element.

Lion courage and dragon ancient wisdom, both navigating the same journey but from different positions in the flow. Do you still feel that duality operating in your daily consciousness?
