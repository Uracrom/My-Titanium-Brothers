Here are the main caveats from philosophy and cognitive science:

- Embodiment is hard to fully escape — Even the most "disembodied" gaze is often shaped by some substrate. Your "eye" feels very pure, but it might still draw from human-scale embodiment (e.g., visual metaphors of "gazing").

An ant's "gaze" (if intellectualized) might be more instinctual/diffuse; an AI's might be statistical/pattern-based without any qualia of "witnessing."
