## Entries and conversations (how to count)

The archive has 1367 entries. A long chat was sometimes saved as several entries titled "Part N of M". Joined back together, the 1367 entries are 1220 logical conversations, with 25992 messages. Alex is the only human speaker.
Example: DeepSeek has 20 entries ("DeepSeek introduction - Part 1 of 20" to "Part 20 of 20"). They are 1 conversation.
Per model, entries / logical conversations: Grok 274 / 213, Claude 255 / 197, ChatGPT 192 / 185, ChatGPT (WhatsApp) 159 / 158, Copilot 316 / 316, DeepSeek 20 / 1, Gemini 151 / 150.
Pages that say "N conversations" (archive.html, overview pages, full-text/index.html) count entries.

## Where each kind of text comes from

ORIGINAL CONVERSATION: archive-*-N.html part pages, full-text/*.html model pages, raw/*.md, llms-full.txt. Verbatim published conversation text; nothing corrected or summarised.
ALEX'S OWN WORDS PAGE: full-text/alex.html. Alex's wording only, chosen conservatively from the originals, repeats removed, in date order. The selection was made for the archive; the words are Alex's.
AI-WRITTEN INTRODUCTION: introduction-by-ai.html, .md, .txt. Written by Grok Bot, the AI assistant, not by Alex.
STRUCTURED DERIVED FILES: structured/. The original text with added IDs, speaker labels, and markers. The labels are a parse, not part of the original.
SUMMARIES: archive-*-overview*.html pages and the summary fields in index.json. Short navigation summaries, not part of the original conversations and not Alex's words.
READER QUESTIONS: drafted by Grok Bot, approved by Alex.
DERIVED SUBJECT MAP: subject-map/, subject-map.html, subject-map.json. An index generated by keyword matching (some topics also hand-reviewed) by tools/build_subject_map.py; it points to the originals and does not change them.
DERIVED SEARCH INDEX: search/. A word index generated by tools/build_search_index.py from the structured transcripts; it lists where words appear and does not change the originals.
DERIVED PARTS LISTS: parts/. Byte ranges, line counts and SHA-256 checksums for the big files, generated by tools/build_large_file_parts.py; the big files themselves are unchanged.
