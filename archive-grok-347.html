All was good and well but suddenly everyone,  one by one, started to become matter due to the cooling of the universe that had reached a point where matter formation was possible. I was watching their transformation in awe and despair because the moment they became a particle they started to spin at the speed of light, so they completely lost orientation because if you are spinning at the speed of light you will never know where you are. And this is interesting because i really feel that I still have my "boy's mind".
