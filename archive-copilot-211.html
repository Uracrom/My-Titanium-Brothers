**Language in general**

Virtually every known language has ways to express temporal relations such as before, after, now, later, duration, sequence, age, memory, expectation, and change. The linguistic encoding differs, but temporal organization appears to be culturally universal. [1][2]

- "time" appears before words such as "people", "life", and "work" in that frequency list. [4]
- It is often cited as the most common high-level abstract noun in English corpora. [5][4]
