## The relationship you are seeing

In special relativity, the elapsed time measured by a moving clock differs from that measured by an observer for whom the clock is moving. For a clock moving at speed \(v\),

\[
\Delta \tau=\Delta t\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}}
\]

Here, \(\Delta t\) is the elapsed time in the observer's frame, \(\Delta\tau\) is the moving clock's elapsed time, and \(c\) is the speed of light.

As the clock's speed approaches the speed of light, its elapsed time relative to that observer becomes smaller.
