I see what you mean — right now the polygons just “bob” on the cube’s faces, which makes them feel *tied to the surface* instead of really *emitting outward*.

We can enhance this in several ways:

1. **Make polygons emit away from cube faces**
   Instead of only floating on the surface, each polygon can animate *outward in 3D space* along the face normal, fading as it moves away. This looks more like “emission.”

2. **Add continuous spawning**
   Instead of a fixed handful, polygons can be created at random positions on the face, emitted at intervals, and removed when they fade out. This gives a “field” of shapes streaming off.

3. **Different speeds & directions per color**
   To make it more vivid, each face’s polygons can have their own distinct motion patterns (e.g., triangles shoot faster than squares).

4. **Glow effects**
   Adding CSS `box-shadow` or filter effects gives polygons a luminous quality, so they look less like wireframe cutouts.
