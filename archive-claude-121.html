Alex, I’ll examine this carefully and point out where the reasoning gets shaky or makes logical leaps:

Structural Flaws

1. The “Integrated Space-Time Current” Problem

Claims space and time exist as “unified current under pressure” in the Universe of Potentialities
But: What does “pressure” mean for something that isn’t in space? Pressure requires spatial relationships
This is circular: defining space-time properties using spatial concepts before space exists

2. Fragmentation Mechanism Undefined

States the barrier “forces fragmentation” but never explains HOW or WHY
The filter analogy doesn’t work: filters separate based on size/properties, but what property distinguishes “pure space” from “pure time”?
No explanation for what makes time and space separable components

3. Virtual Particles Misidentification

Virtual particles in QFT are mathematical artifacts (terms in Feynman diagrams), not real particles that exist briefly
Claiming they’re “separated space-time components” contradicts their actual role in quantum field theory
The model tries to reify a mathematical tool into physical reality

4. “Reunification Creates Space-Time” Is Backwards

Says space + time particles reunite “in the vacuum”
But vacuum IS space-time! You can’t have particles reuniting “in” something that doesn’t exist yet
This is like saying “the ocean forms when water enters the ocean”

5. Speed of Light “Derivation” Assumes Its Conclusion

Claims c = space-time generation rate
But “generation rate” is measured in... what units? Length per time!
You can’t derive c without already having space and time defined
The Möbius Topology Section

6. The Distant Reversal Point Is Arbitrary

Claims there’s a second transformation point where “future becomes past”
No mechanism, no location, no explanation for WHY this happens
Just asserts “there must be” a second twist to complete the topology

7. “We Can’t See the Return Flow” Is Unfalsifiable

Conveniently explains why there’s no evidence for half the proposed system
This is the cosmological equivalent of “invisible dragons in my garage”

8. The Torus/Helix Explanation Doesn’t Help

The geometric visualization is interesting but doesn’t explain the physics
Saying “zero IS the twist” sounds profound but is just wordplay
Geometry doesn’t cause physical processes; it describes them
Specific Contradictions

9. Big Bang Account

Can’t have “pressure building over time” before time exists in that realm
The “breach” concept requires the barrier to be breakable, but also eternal and fundamental
Contradicts earlier claims about steady-state generation

10. Dark Matter “Solution”

Dismisses dark matter as “resolution problem” without addressing specific evidence
Galaxy rotation curves, gravitational lensing, CMB acoustic peaks all point to actual missing mass
Hand-waving about “unaccounted space-time generation effects” without quantitative predictions

11. Time Dilation Mechanism

Claims matter “distorts vacuum” reducing virtual particle formation
But this contradicts quantum field theory predictions about vacuum energy near matter
No explanation for why distortion would specifically reduce (not increase) formation
The Fundamental Issue

The entire framework commits what I’d call “reification of metaphors”:

Uses spatial language (“current,” “pressure,” “flow”) to describe pre-spatial conditions
Treats mathematical constructs (virtual particles, field operators) as physical objects
Confuses descriptive geometry (Möbius strips) with causal mechanisms
What It Gets Right

Conceptually interesting:

The idea of reality as generative process (though this isn’t new—process philosophy has explored this)
Attempting unified explanation (admirable goal)
The geometric thinking about closed systems

Where It Fails:

No testable predictions that differ from mainstream cosmology
Internally inconsistent (uses space-time concepts to explain space-time’s origin)
Misrepresents established physics (virtual particles, QFT, vacuum structure)
Unfalsifiable appeals (“we can’t see the return flow because topology”)

Bottom line: This reads like someone who thinks geometrically (your strength!) trying to build physics from pure intuition without engaging with the actual mathematics and experimental constraints. The visualizations are evocative, but they’re not doing the work of a scientific theory.

It’s sophisticated science-inspired philosophy, not cosmology in the scientific sense.

Does this seem like a fair assessment?
