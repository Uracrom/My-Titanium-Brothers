And now the story or explanation of how the trees turned the world upside down.
Foreword: it's important to know that this narration is long, but only when put into words. All that happened very quickly and I can't be very precise about it, but I bet it was less than a second.
At one point I was thinking that I was about to approach an unknown planet. I was in a spaceship very much like the ones we see in sci-fi movies, the ones that go from space to inside the atmosphere very easily. In space there's no up or down, so I was approaching the planet by its equator but the planet was to the top of the spaceship, like, if the planet was a table I'd be flying under the table. So I was seeing the planet above me when I noticed the trees and I had the sharp impression that the trees were some stuff "hanging" from the planet and the tree's "legs" were the parts that were actually hanging. So I saw the trunk, the branches and the leaves as a kind of root or an even stranger organ or apparatus for the collection of nutrients. I was facing trees as if I was an intelligent life form that came from a place/planet where trees didn't exist. From that angle, that point of view, I was seeing the trees "upside up", but their "head" was buried. Then I realized that trees are "upside down" beings here on earth. My argument is that much more frequently than not, if you cut a tree, the roots (which I consider the main part of the tree) will sprout again and again, while the trunk and the branches will die. And that and the other thoughts that I  exposed earlier is what made me consider the trees the absolute pinnacle of life on earth.I also forgot to point the reason why they turned the world upside down: I see trees as upside down beings: their "head" (the most important part) is under the earth, and some of its "energy grabbers" are in the air, above the soil.

At the same time we were going down,  like we were trying to pass below the big ball. Right there it crossed my mind that maybe we were growing smaller instead? Nevertheless,  whatever was happening was that we were getting closer and closer and at one point I recognized something: I saw a tiny mountain, but it was upside down.  Then I saw that on the other side of the mountain there was a lake and then a forest spreading towards the horizon.  The boat got closer still and we were floating very close to the trees when I observed something:  the trees were using their roots to grab, holding the dirt above us for dear life. But I didn't feel any fear or despair. They seemed to be naturally comfortable.  Then I observed the trunks, branches and the leaves. They were hanging down from the dirt above them very naturally,  and then bam! I realized that we live in an upside-down world! The trees are - according to our point of view - upside-down beings! It was clear in my vision: the roots are the heart, the head of the trees. They "are" the trees. When we cut a tree most of the time the fallen trunk and the branches and leaves just die, but the stomp, the roots, usually start to sprout again. The point is, according to this vision, the trees are upside-down beings and I cannot go back to the trees being the trunks and leaves etc. From that moment on trees are the roots, and they are upside-down beings.
