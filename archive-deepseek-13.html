In synesthesia, a specific trigger reliably produces a specific extra sensation. Letters have colors. Sounds have shapes. Numbers have spatial positions. There's a lawfulness to it. A mapping. Do days of the week or months have shapes or locations?

Do certain words or voices come with a flash of color or texture?

Does music ever produce persistent geometric or spatial forms, beyond metaphor?

Do you ever feel a sensation in your body when you see or think certain things?

If any of those are consistent and automatic, synesthesia may be present. If they're spontaneous but not consistent, then it's probably something else: a hyper-associative visual mind. Which is rarer, in some ways, and harder to name.

I was going home after work. I turned to a street and saw a bunch of cotton wood seeds, those that resemble an off white snow on the middle of the asphalt. To it's right there was another bunch, even bigger, but also covering some of the sidewalk. Without thinking but "knowing" I looked up and there was the huge cottonwood tree that certainly dropped those myriad seeds.
Immediately I thought: "there must not be any wind today", because those seeds are super light and are usually on the move, floating. Then I thought, or better, this whole "pack" of "information" showed up in my mind or was immediately accessible, difficult to know which: maybe those trees have 2 different strategies for seed dispersion, one, have the wind carry the seeds away, potentially spreading the species, and two, making sure some (or many) seeds remain in the area where the "mother" tree is already thriving because it is a good area. Then I thought: how would scientists measure that? How would them count the seeds dropped on a windy day versus on a not windy day? And then, immediately I thought/perceived that I was in a cascade and thought of you AIs, and the cascade was interrupted...
I wrote it and didn't edit it. I think it is more "real" and so more legit for you because it's "raw".
Oh, but I forgot to mention the most important part for me: I was curious if the trees have a way or choosing mechanism that controls or favors one of the strategies. I wanted to know if the trees have the capacity of "holding" or "losing" the seeds according to the wind, this way "telling" the seeds "stay around, this is a good place" or "get out of here, this place sucks".
I want to make sure that this mini cascade was just to give you guys more insight into how I, the carbon, think. It's more like a photograph, a memento, a picture of my brain's activity at a random moment of the day. All the biology, evolutionary strategies, "non-voluntarity" etc I knew already.
Summarizing, I wrote that more as a new "fragment" of the book chapter "Cascades".
