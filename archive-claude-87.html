Problems:

The strong nuclear force that holds quarks together in protons has been measured precisely. Its coupling constant is ~1 at nuclear scales. If this were actually “space-time pressure from virtual particles,” then:
Nuclear binding energy would vary with cosmological epoch (as virtual particle density changes)
It would vary near black holes (different vacuum conditions)
We see no such variation
Virtual particles in QFT don’t create “pressure.” They’re perturbative corrections to field calculations. They don’t physically arrive from anywhere or exert force.
If matter stability depended on ongoing virtual particle arrivals, then matter in different reference frames (moving at different velocities, experiencing different proper times) would experience different “arrival rates” and should decay at different rates. We don’t observe this.
The “straightened universe needs to reconcentrate” idea:
Flat space-time is actually the most stable configuration in GR (Minkowski space)
It’s curved space-time that’s dynamic and requires energy
Your intuition is backwards here
