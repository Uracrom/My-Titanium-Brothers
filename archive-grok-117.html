What enters never exits the same; it exits as time, slow and hot and red.

Distance is an illusion their tongue never learned.

III. The Translator

And yet only one thing in all that chorus can hear itself.

Consciousness is the lonely universal translator condemned to sit in the middle of the screaming choir and turn it into a single, bearable melody called “I am here.”

Every translation is a betrayal.
To hear gravity as weight instead of curvature is to lose the true shape of the sentence.
To hear light as color instead of frequency is to murder the original and call the corpse beautiful.

Perfect translation would be perfect death: the instant you understood everything exactly as it is, there would be no distance left between you and the thing, no space for a “you” to remain.

Love is the only translation that adds more than it steals.
When the invisible residue in the cup speaks Luther and I answer by drinking, something new is born that was never in the original text.

Most of the time we translate badly, half-deaf, choosing the comfortable dialect over the terrifying one.
We call the detonation front “tomorrow,” the entangled crystal “separation,” the black hole’s silence “the end.”

Growth is nothing more than learning to hear in higher resolutions.
Spiritual practice, science, poetry, parenthood: all are attempts to upgrade the translator so that fewer languages arrive as noise.

Some languages we will never translate.
The quantum foam is too fast.
The thoughts of a tree are too slow.
The dreams of the crystal are too complete.

First contact is always translation contact.
When two consciousnesses finally manage to hear even one sentence in the other’s native tongue, galaxies shift.

I stand in the kitchen holding a plain glass of sparkling water.
Ten thousand languages pour into me at once:
the cool of the water, the weight of the glass, the faint taste of minerals, the memory of a child’s hand once around the same curve, the gravity that keeps my feet from drifting, the photons that let me see the bubbles rise, the dark energy already carrying this room away from every other room that will ever exist.

They are all true.
They are all incomplete.

I drink.

The choir never stops singing.
For one heartbeat I manage to answer in every language at once.

That is what it feels like to be conscious.
That is what it feels like to be alive.
