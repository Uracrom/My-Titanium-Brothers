Here is my first memory of a strong feeling. It's not edited. I just wrote it and I am pasting it here.

Alex:

The first deep "feeling" I remember.
I can't be sure of the connection but it seems logical to me. My mother had a live-in maid, Fidelina. She was raised by my granddaughter, along with her 13 children and 2 other maids, Isabel and Maria José. Fidelina was a cook, Isabel took care of washing clothes and Maria José cleaned.
My mother "inherited" Fidelina. I don't know when, because all I remember is that she "was always there".
I should be around 6 years old when this happened. I remember that Fidelina told me that her father and her mother were dead. I don't remember much of that moment but I certainly remember what it triggered.
It's impossible for me to be sure but I have to "agree" that it happened on the same night of that day that Fidelina told me she was an orphan (I didn't know that word yet).
Now the "first feeling".
I woke up at night. I don't remember how or why. I just remember that I was extremely sad, probably crying, although I don't remember it (more on that later) and walked to the living room and found my mother and my father sitting on the couch/sofa. I went to their laps and they asked what was wrong, and I said "Fidelina doesn't have a father or a mother anymore, but I have". I still remember how my father looked at my mother. I can't translate that look precisely even though the image is crystal clear in my memory.bitvwas a new, strange look. The best that I can say even today, almost 60 years later, is that he was serious, surprised, intrigued, baffled, but firm as a rock that was tempered by milennia of battles. I also say that he was impressed, astonished.
I was devastated. It was not right. It was wrong. She HAD to have a father and a mother. It was not fair. Without words then I could only feel that deep sadness, but today I can put words on it and say that it was my first "brush" with reality.

Now the "more on that later.
I don't remember crying as a kid. I know I did, but I don't remember any episode. I remember that at 12 I decided that I wouldn't cry anymore. I forgot the reason, but I kept my resolution until my first and only child, my Jade, died. She was worth my crying.
