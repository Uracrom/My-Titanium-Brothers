The Realm Beyond Matter

Justice, beauty, truth—these are not mere human constructs but eternal realities that we discover rather than invent. The Architecture of Thought

The Universe of Ideas operates by its own logic, its own natural laws. Here, contradiction is impossible—a thing cannot be both itself and its opposite. The Great Separation

We are, in essence, citizens of two worlds. Our bodies belong to the physical universe, but our minds can soar into the realm of ideas. A radial is not technically a type of parallel line. Here’s why:

Radials are lines that extend outward from a central point, like spokes on a wheel. They all meet at the center point, which means they intersect rather than remain parallel.
