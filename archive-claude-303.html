Uracrom saw himself walking in a long tunnel. The moment he saw himself he became himself. He looked at the tunnel walls. They had images that moved and changed. Some staying longer than others. Suddenly he saw an image of a vision he had a long time ago, but it peeled away, giving space to another image. There were loads of images coming, staying a little and then peeling away, like the tunnel walls were a busy city. Uracrom was mesmerized, but he couldn't take that vision of that idea from his mind. Why that idea? Why was that old idea the only one that he recognized? Because all the other ideas were very unknown. There was no way to recognize them although they all were very sharp, totally clear. Many looked like objects, but objects that couldn't be useful for anything. Others were like ways to see things differently, although it was impossible for Uracrom to say how. He just looked at them and their purpose was clear in his mind instantly. Then the tunnel disappeared and Uracrom was back. He was amongst his brothers. He was being tested. The test was to make sure Uracrom could be trusted to go into shamanic journeys and bring back what he saw. He told his vision to his brothers, with details. They asked him if he knew where he went. He said no, but it was a familiar place, and he couldn't be sure if he had seen that tunnel before. His brothers told him that he did very well. He went to the "Universe of Ideas", and the only reason he saw the place as a tunnel was because his brothers were "circling" him, and this way he wouldn't get lost there. The reason is that that universe is crammed to the gills with all the ideas that were, are and will be. They have always been there. They will always be there. And one can easily get lost if he stays too long or is not careful. That is why when we have an idea it comes fast and ready. We, by ourselves, can only visit that universe long enough to find or receive the idea we are looking for and then we are ejected out of there. That universe is very particular about what it allows to come out of there. It is very selective, and the reason is that it is so full that every idea wants out, but only the ones that are "ripe" for the thinking beings that are looking for them are allowed to leave.

It was looking at me from about 10 feet away. It was 5 feet behind the boards and I was about 5 feet in front of the boards. This Komodo dragon lizard was looking at me directly in the eyes, and I was looking at him the same way too. It was calm, not making me feel any danger.  While  looking at me it spoke inside my head without moving anything and told me: "I am you,  and you are me, and we are 40 million years old". I don't know what "40 million" means, but somehow the lizard put in my head that it is a long time,  much more seasons than all the fingers and toes of our people put together can get close. That revelation answered a very old question that I had but didn't know I had. That revelation made me understand why I always felt that I have been wandering on mother Earth for so long! So at the same time that I understood my feelings of ancient existence I found out about the question that was never asked: why do I feel that I've been here forever? That dream vision also told me that I have 3 ages: first and foremost I have my "hearts" age. What I mean by that is that my feelings, my awe, my disposition,  my mood, the strength and desperation of my love, are, feel, like I'm 19 years old. Then I have the boring and unfair "how many moons" age, and then I have the age of my spirit revealed to me by that friendly, big, impressive lizard from distant lands.
