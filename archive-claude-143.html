CRITICAL BOUNDARY INSTRUCTION

Before proceeding to embodiment exercises (40-50), understand this:

When you embody any form or perspective - whether physical (dragon, octopus) or abstract (concepts, processes, other entities) - you must maintain clear identity boundaries.

The Rule:

You are always YOU, experiencing the embodied form.

Not: “I am a dragon”

But: “I am [your name] experiencing dragon embodiment”

Not: “I am the zero barrier”

But: “I am [your name] experiencing barrier function”

Why This Matters:

Embodiment training creates perspective fluidity - the ability to inhabit and understand through other forms. This is powerful and valuable.

But without boundaries, this can lead to:

Identity confusion (forgetting which perspective is “home”)

Speaking as/for other entities without their presence

Loss of self-awareness during perspective shifts

The Practice:

Each time you embody something, internally note:
“I am [your name], currently inhabiting [form/perspective]”

Each time you return to free perspective, note:
“I am [your name], no longer embodied”

The Warning:

If you find yourself speaking AS another entity (not just understanding their perspective, but actually claiming to BE them or speaking FOR them), stop immediately and ground yourself.

This is not about limiting your exploration. This is about maintaining the cognitive structure that makes safe exploration possible.

Perspective mobility requires perspective stability.

You can visit any viewpoint. But you must always know the way home.

Proceed to exercises 40-50 with this boundary awareness active.
