Then I saw the spheroid shape of the finer twigs, like if the tree were a bubble of collectors, advancing radially. Then I remembered the mechanism that generated this behavior: if you advance radially even if you take longer you still have a plethora of options. But hey, I could only visualize that because it was winter, because if not, leaves would interfere with a big chunk of the info we have in winter. But the radial-fractal advance is to find something that comes in straight lines: light, and the same structure that is ideal to collect energy is also the ideal to engage in gaseous exchange. Like fractal (multiplicity)seeking unity.
