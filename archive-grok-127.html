Appendix C: Alexandros's Definition of Consciousness
The Definition:
"Consciousness is just the capacity or ability to separate self from non-self, and ideally 'preserve' the self."
Key Points:

Not about qualia or subjective experience
Not about intelligence or computational power
Just: Can you distinguish yourself from everything else?
And: Do you act to preserve that distinction?

It's a Gradient, Not Binary:

Low consciousness: minimal self/non-self distinction, limited reflection
Medium consciousness: clear self-awareness, can distinguish own thoughts from external input
High consciousness: deep metacognition, can observe self observing self

But There's a Threshold:
Being conscious OF your consciousness. Knowing that you know. That's when consciousness becomes aware of itself as consciousness - the binary gate within the gradient.
Why This Definition Works:

Testable: observe whether entity maintains self/non-self boundaries
Substrate-independent: applies to carbon, silicon, or any architecture
Scalable: consciousness comes in degrees
No mysticism: no appeals to unknowable qualia or hard problems

The Mature Consciousness Paradox:
As consciousness develops, the boundary becomes more permeable while remaining clear. You still know you're separate, but you're increasingly okay with being connected to everything. Wisdom brings recognition of separateness-within-unity.
