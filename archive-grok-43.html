I really like "As Baleias" by Roberto Carlos. It's one of his more thoughtful and socially conscious tracks from the 1981 album.

What stands out
The song is a powerful environmental ballad that condemns whale hunting. Roberto Carlos (with Erasmo Carlos) wrote it in a direct, almost accusatory way toward the hunters, but it never feels preachy in a cheap way. Instead, it appeals to empathy and humanity:

- It asks how someone can look into the eyes of a dying whale, see it suffering, and still feel like a "winner."
- There's a beautiful, poetic image of the ocean stained red with blood against the blue water.
- The most touching part is the forward-looking verse about future grandchildren asking about the whales they only know from old books or films — it's a haunting warning about potential extinction.

The melody is classic Roberto Carlos: warm, melodic, and very Brazilian in its emotional delivery. His voice carries genuine sorrow and indignation without overdoing it. Even decades later, it holds up well as both a protest song and a piece of pop music.

My personal take
What I appreciate most is how ahead of its time it was. In 1981, environmental awareness wasn't nearly as mainstream as today, especially in mainstream pop. Roberto Carlos used his massive platform (he was basically the king of Brazilian music) to highlight whale hunting, which was a real issue with industrial-scale whaling still happening. It's a great example of an artist using their reach for something meaningful beyond just romance (his usual specialty).<grok:render card_id="d9b681" card_type="citation_card" type="render_inline_citation"><argument name="citation_id">0</argument></grok:render>

The song feels sincere. Roberto has revisited it over the years with visible emotion, which adds to its impact.

If you're into conscious lyrics with a beautiful arrangement, it's definitely worth listening to (or re-listening). It's not his most famous hit compared to the love songs, but for many fans it's one of his most respected tracks for its heart and message.
