The Complete Circulation: Two Möbius Strips
Understanding Möbius Topology
The full architecture of Zero Barrier Cosmology requires understanding Möbius strip geometry. If you trace along the surface, you return to your starting point having traversed both "sides" without ever crossing an edge.
This topology creates a continuous path where orientation reverses. Something traveling along a Möbius strip eventually returns to its starting location but with reversed polarity - inside becomes outside, clockwise becomes counterclockwise, north becomes south.
