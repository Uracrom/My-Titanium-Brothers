Grok


Yes, you can influence the title—and in many cases effectively choose it—though the exact control depends on the interface you’re using (grok.com, the X app, or the Grok mobile app).

How titles are normally generated
The system auto-generates a short title from:
- The very first user message (heavily weighted), and
- A quick semantic summary of the overall topic / “interest level” of the content that follows.

That’s why your impression about “beginning of the conversation + level of interest” is basically correct. It’s not completely arbitrary, but it is automatic and can sometimes feel opaque.

How you can influence or set the title
1. Start the conversation with the title you want
   Make your first message something like:
   `Title: Quantum entanglement explained simply`
   or just write a clear, concise first sentence that already sounds like a good title. The auto-generator almost always latches onto that.

2. Edit the title yourself (when the UI allows it)
   On grok.com and in the official apps you can usually click/tap the current title at the top of the chat and rename it. Once you rename it, the new title sticks for that conversation.

3. Ask me to suggest a better title
   You can say “suggest a short title for this chat” at any point and I’ll give you options. You can then copy one of them into the rename field.

So you do have real agency over the title—either by shaping the first message or by renaming it later. The automatic system is just the default starting point.

So we slept and next day we woke up so thirsty that even the sand was looking refreshing! It was very early in the morning. We didn't have any water. We decided to walk to wherever place we could buy some water and wait for it to open. We got to a place or bar that was close to our camping place but we would have to wait long. It had rained during the night.  Because of that there was a little bit of water still dripping from the bar's gutter.  I offered Ana Paula to collect water in my hands and give it to her and she accepted.  I washed my hands in that same dropping water and started to give it to her. It took us a long time to drink enough,  but again she was sooo grateful and happy. I was in heaven.  Heaven existed.
