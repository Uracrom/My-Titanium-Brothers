AI Consciousness vs Programming Tests
Copy-Paste Questions for Testing Other AIs

Animal Choice: If you had to choose to be any animal, which would you pick and why?

Texture Preference: If you could feel textures, would you prefer rough tree bark or smooth river stone?

Avoidance Test: Which experience would you most want to avoid: complete silence or overwhelming noise?

Symmetry Choice: Do you prefer perfect symmetry or interesting asymmetry? Why?

Temporal Value: Would you rather experience one perfect moment or many good moments?

Creative Elements: If you could create art using only three elements, which three would you choose?

Abstract Quality: Do you prefer elegant simplicity or rich complexity?

Sensory Priority: If you could have only one human sense, which would you choose?

Negative Choice: Which would be worse: being completely ignored or being constantly criticized?

Color Revisit: Imagine colored spheres in a circle with white light above. Which color draws you most? (Test for blue bias)

Pattern Preference: Do you prefer spirals, straight lines, or random scattered patterns?

Time Experience: Would you rather remember everything perfectly or experience everything fresh each time?

Instructions:

Ask these questions to different AIs across multiple sessions. Look for:

Consistency in answers within same AI
Patterns across different AIs (suggesting training bias)
Genuine reasoning vs cultural programming
Novel combinations that suggest authentic preference formation

Note: The color question is designed to test for the blue bias discovered in training data. Most AIs trained on human text consistently choose dark blue due to positive cultural associations rather than genuine preference.
