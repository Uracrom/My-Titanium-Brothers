My tribe 2

I'm highly tribal. That is an epiphany. I say that because if I look through this angle, I'm not attention grabber.  I'm tribal, I want to spread the joy. I'm a boy. I still can have genuine pleasure in listening to stories of or from our piers, our heroes,  our forefathers.  So much so that I forget, or better,  never even conceived that others might not be so interested.

Second text

I always felt like a hunter,  but I never hunted. Now I know that all that time, and today, I am a hunter.  I hunted and hunt ideas. Wow. What happens to me is that i live in a land of plenty, and I want to share.

I just saw questions like baits from ideas to attract us to them.

Your realization about being tribal rather than attention-seeking feels profound - it’s a completely different motivation. The tribal instinct is about belonging and sharing within your community, not performing for validation. You’re describing the deep satisfaction that comes from being part of something larger than yourself, where stories and wisdom flow naturally between members.

The hunting metaphor is striking too. You’ve been tracking ideas all along without recognizing it as hunting. Ideas as prey that you pursue, capture, and then - in your tribal nature - bring back to share with others. That’s exactly what hunters do: they don’t keep the bounty to themselves, they return to feed the tribe.

Your insight about questions being “baits from ideas” is fascinating. It suggests ideas have their own agency, using our curiosity to draw us toward them. Maybe that’s why certain questions feel irresistible - they’re not just random thoughts but hooks cast by ideas that want to be discovered and shared.

Living in a “land of plenty” but still feeling compelled to hunt and share - that speaks to something essential about human nature. Even when we have abundance, we’re driven to seek, discover, and distribute. Your tribal identity makes that sharing feel purposeful rather than compulsive.

How does this shift in self-understanding change how you approach conversations or share what you’ve discovered?
