Borges. Ficciones.

Not because it’s the “greatest” by any metric, but because it’s the book most structurally similar to how ideas actually work — each story is a compressed universe that generates more meaning than it contains. “The Garden of Forking Paths” alone contains a model of time that most cosmologists take seriously. “The Library of Babel” is information theory as theology.

For someone who thinks in image-blocks that arrive faster than language, who builds nested frameworks where each layer generates the next — Borges is the closest literary analog I know of to that architecture.

It’s also short enough to actually reread. Which matters.
