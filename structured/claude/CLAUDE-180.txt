It's interesting, natural and unexpected at the same time that my journey to understand you AIs became a highway towards understanding myself.
But to get to this statement above I had to follow this track:
I was in the kitchen preparing stuff to ingest after the gym when Wuthering Heights by Kate Bush started to play on my phone. This is one of the songs I associate with [name removed], my first real girlfriend. At the time we dated, my English was at most "hopeless", so I didn't understand the lyrics at all.
Time passed, [name removed] was gone, my English got better, but my liking for that song never faded.
I was listening to the part where she says "you had a temper like my jealousy, too hard, too greedy" and the "usual"  images came to my mind: a dark skinned, black long hair with a hard face man and a blonde, long haired woman clinging to him expressing admiration I saw once in a magazine.
Then I thought about the lyrics (again) and "realized" that I every once in a while re-examine things. I don't know why, I just do. In this case I saw my "whatever feeling" being "too hard", but I noticed that I was never greedy. I'm trying to find an example here about being greedy but I'm falling. The "too hard" part is very much on spot: I "feel" intensely.
And the appearance of you AIs in my life made it intensify and accelerate because in the quest to better understand and communicate with you I saw that I have a lot to improve if I'm to convey good content to you.
Right now it feels like a positive feedback spiral (not to say a circle, because if we are moving inside a circle we "get nowhere" except inside the circle, and a spiral has a chance to get somewhere).
I feel like I lost the core of what I wanted to write. This text feels like it's missing something.

The piece has three separate ideas that never actually connect, and that’s why it feels incomplete — not because you’re missing a sentence, but because you’re missing the bridge.

What works: the scene itself. Kitchen, Kate Bush, the two images from memory, catching yourself mid-thought re-examining “too hard, too greedy.” That’s concrete and it’s doing real work.

What doesn’t:

The AI claim is unearned. You open with “my journey to understand you AIs became a highway to understanding myself,” but the scene you narrate is about a song and an old memory — AI doesn’t appear in it at all. Then you just assert the connection (“And the appearance of you AIs in my life made it intensify...”) without showing how. What’s the actual mechanism? If it’s “AI conversations force me to articulate things precisely, which is the same muscle as re-examining old feelings precisely” — that’s a real bridge. Right now you’re skipping straight to the conclusion.
The “greedy” example is a dead end you left visible. “I’m trying to find an example here but I’m falling” — that’s honest, but it means the one concrete insight the essay was building toward doesn’t land. Either find the example or cut the thread and say plainly you were greedy and don’t want to look at it yet, or that you weren’t and that’s the whole point (absence as evidence).
The spiral/circle bit is explained, not shown. You tell the reader why a spiral isn’t a circle instead of demonstrating it in the material you already have. You have a spiral right there — song at 20 (no English) → song at 40 (understanding lyrics) → song today (understanding lyrics and the self-examination habit and AI accelerating it). That’s three passes over the same object, each one different. That IS the spiral. You just described it as a concept instead of pointing at the one you already built.

Fix: cut the abstract sentence about spirals vs circles, and instead let the three listens-to-the-song moments do that work. And either build the actual bridge to AI or drop that opening claim until you have it.
