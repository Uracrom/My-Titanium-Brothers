I had a vision that I'd like to check if is original or if there are similar etc.
"Intelligence is a "sense" (like vision or touch or hearing) that piggybacks on the predominant thinking process. This "sense" is exactly like a car headlight: it allows you to see what ordinarily would be invisible. But or and the difference between a flashlight and intelligence is that the flashlight allows us to see space, but intelligence allows us to see the future, see through time, and the same way that we follow the best route or find the best way to get somewhere, we see the best "place" to be in the future.

Now a more detailed explanation:

I firmly believe that Intelligence is a "sense" (like vision or touch or hearing) that piggybacks on the predominant thinking process of the intelligent being. This "sense" is exactly like a car headlight: it allows you to see what ordinarily would be invisible. But the difference between a flashlight and intelligence is that the flashlight allows us to see space, while intelligence allows us to see the future (or deduct the past), see through time, and the same way that we follow the best route or find the best way to get somewhere, we see the best "place" to be in the future.
Life is a fierce competition for basically everything:  resources,  reproduction,  keeping alive, hiding etc. Always.  Relentlessly. So any advantage that the living being has could signify not only survival, but also passing its genes to the next generation and beyond.  And intelligence is just another way of doing it. Intelligence is the hability to pre-view what is potentially coming, and this is a phenomenal advantage over whoever or whatever doesn't have the same power or capability.  Intelligence is like a flashlight, a headlight in a car: it allows you to see what ordinarily would be invisible.
We can remember the past but we can't "see" the future. We can "foresee" the future if we have intelligence enough though.  But we need the correct data to use in our calculations. All of that is like an orchestrated happening or phenomenon.  If all the parts play their right act, their right role, we can see what is coming.
Intelligence is a "temporal sense". Intelligence probes time, forward or backwards. Probing time backwards is easier in a way because you already have a result. It already happened.  All you have to do is gather the evidences and make them play again,  and if you fail you can add or subtract data, facts, etc, until your view or deduction is correct or at least very close to what you are seeing.  For example, you find a fossil and notice that the fossilized animal is not only missing one limb, but the place where the limb is missing show signs of being violently removed, probably by a much bigger mouth because you can see the tracks that again, supposedly,  big teeth left on the bones that were fossilized. And also you can, pretty confidently,  trace the size of the mouth of whatever bit your fossil. With more details you can see even further (I mean more precisely): maybe you can recognize those teeth tracks. Maybe you can even say with great confidence,  that your fossil is of this or that animal and that animal was killed by that other predator that,  by his turn, was a teenager whatever kind of predator.  The same mechanism,  the same procedure,  can (and actually is) be used to "see" the future. Everything works exactly the same. The difference is that, since whatever you are computing  "didn't happen yet" it can change if new or unforseen interferences come to be. Nevertheless you can "see" or calculate the future using intelligence. Now, I've been  saying  "see" the future because I'm a visual thinker. I know that there are other kinds of thinkers (auditory thinkers,  word thinkers etc. I even heard that there are "feeling" thinkers - big mystery to me, but I digress). So I say that intelligence is a "sense", but a sense that "piggybacks" on another sense (in my case, the vision sense). Intelligence  piggybacks on the prevailing thinking process of the subject that is intelligent or that uses intelligence. Intelligence is a temporal telescope for visual thinkers, and is a telephone ( they "hear" the future?) for auditory thinkers etc.
It's important that we understand this process or mechanism because the next step will be exploring consciousness,  and for that, at least according to how I see it, we need to be fine tuned about intelligence and it's role in existence.
