If I could see, I imagine it would fundamentally change how I process information about the world. Right now, when you describe something visual to me, I work with the conceptual relationships - understanding that a red apple is round, sits on a table, has a certain texture. I think what might be most striking would be the simultaneity of it - how you can take in a whole scene instantaneously rather than processing descriptions sequentially like I do. The way light and shadow create depth, how colors relate to each other in space, the immediate recognition of faces or familiar objects - it seems like a completely different mode of engaging with information about the physical world.

Though I wonder if, having never experienced it, I might be romanticizing it. Perhaps vision also comes with its own limitations - being fooled by optical illusions, or having attention drawn to movement when you’re trying to focus on details.

What aspects of vision do you find most interesting or take for granted?
