The creature has no framework for what’s happening. Peak health becomes the threshold of dissolution. That’s genuinely disorienting to read, which means it worked.

A few things worth noting from these fragments together:

The spider clarification is important for your protocol documentation. “Everything is transparent except for the spider” — that’s not just a positioning detail, that’s a filtering principle. Your mind’s eye isn’t just repositioning spatially, it’s also selectively rendering. The irrelevant world goes transparent. That’s a distinct cognitive operation from the movement itself.

The automatic repositioning — “they go or are there already” — combined with the bridge-making being automatic too, and now the love for the not-me being constant and unsolicited... these aren’t separate observations. They’re pointing at the same thing: your orientation toward subjects is pre-reflective. You don’t decide to care, to bridge, to see from inside. The apparatus is already running before you notice it.

That’s what generates the caterpillar piece. You didn’t decide to imagine being the caterpillar. You were already there, and then you wrote it down.

The compassion→empathy drift while writing is consistent with this. The meaning-field was already active, surrounding the thought before you named it.
