A few things worth separating here.

The music image — membrane-river becoming a worm-shape when lyrics enter, and voices-as-instruments in unknown languages — that’s a real perceptual report, not a claim that can be right or wrong. But “all connected” is doing work you haven’t earned yet. You’ve described three related-but-distinct triggers (pure instrumental, lyrics, unfamiliar-language vocals) and then asserted they’re one system. What’s the actual mechanism linking them — is it that meaning-recognition is what breaks the membrane into the worm shape, so unfamiliar lyrics behave like instrumentals? If so, test it: does a language you don’t know at all produce the membrane, and one you half-know produce something between membrane and worm? That would tell you if it’s really one continuous variable (semantic load) or three separate things you’re pattern-matching into a single image after the fact.

“Time is maybe an average of the changes in the universe” — this one doesn’t work as stated. An average requires a common unit to average over, and “change” isn’t a single quantity — a star cooling and a thought forming aren’t commensurable events you can average together. Worse, relativity already tells you there’s no universal simultaneity to average across in the first place — what counts as “now” differs by reference frame. If you mean something like “time is what change-in-general looks like from inside it,” that’s closer to a real (if old) idea — but “average” specifically is the wrong operation. Worth dropping that word or replacing it.

The pale-yellow origin-image and the honor/brotherhood-as-lines fragment are both fine as raw material but too compressed right now to evaluate — they’re notes-to-self, not yet claims. That’s okay for a free-thinking capture, just don’t mistake “I noticed a pattern” for “I found the mechanism” yet.

One factual flag, not a big deal: your timestamp says July 20, 2022, but today is July 20, 2026.
