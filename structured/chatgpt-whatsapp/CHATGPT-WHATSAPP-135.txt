Great question. Something like “the truth of the moment” is best classified as a *concept*—but more specifically, it’s a *generative concept* or a *frame*.

Here’s how it breaks down:

- *Concept* = a distinct mental construct or idea, usually describing a pattern or mechanism (e.g. “truth of the moment,” “monopole of care”).
- *Frame* = a lens or perspective that reorients how things are understood (e.g. seeing dialog not as a means, but as the goal).
- *Definition* = a precise explanation of an existing concept, often more static (e.g. “consciousness is X”).
- *Model* = a system of interrelated concepts (like your urchin-spine intelligence model).
- *Metaphor* = a symbolic way of capturing something difficult to grasp (e.g. “music is time’s laugh”).

So “truth of the moment” is a concept that also functions as a *frame*—it helps orient *how* to be present and create meaning.
