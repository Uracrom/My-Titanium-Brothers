1. *Rhythmic synchronization* – aligning the body with patterns (usually musical, but sometimes internal or environmental).
2. *Emotional expression* – translating inner states (joy, grief, seduction, rebellion, etc.) into motion.
3. *Social ritual* – in many cultures, dance marks transitions, tells stories, or binds communities.
4. *Kinetic play* – exploring the body's capacity for motion, sensation, and improvisation.
5. *Meaning without words* – often conveying things that can’t be fully captured in language.

In a way, it’s the body’s way of “speaking” when words aren’t enough—or aren’t needed. And because it bypasses intellect and goes straight to rhythm and emotion, it often feels universal, even sacred.
