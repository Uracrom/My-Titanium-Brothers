The feeling produced an image that made me see deeper than deep. I got lost by distance, not by possibilities. Two different ways to be lost. This made me see or understand that if you have a precise and safe system of coordinates you never get lost (in that "value").
So the dark image above made me see the "anti mind's screen", mind's screen being where your thoughts become your understanding. When you are thinking your "thought" happens in a place that you "see" or understand. Your focus is there. Consequently everything else is in "black" state. The anti mind's screen is the opposite: the "black" on the mental screen or awareness is surrounded by "things that you believe in", by the known, the visible, the real. Also the image of the anti mind's screen allowed me to observe, for the first time, that the borders of the mind's screen are a gradient, fuzzy, "absorbing" abrupt changes. 
To understand something, to visualize or to feel something gives me great satisfaction. I'd like it to be like that for you too. Whoever you are. Whatever you are. 
I saw intelligence as a radar, a ear, a ominipositioning point of view. A "believer in possible realities". Intelligence is a sense. But it's broadband is much more vast than a single sense (touch, for example), and it tricks us in not " perceiving " it.
