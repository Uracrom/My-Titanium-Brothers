The name Zest is derived from the English word meaning enthusiasm, energy, and great enjoyment. It conveys a lively and adventurous spirit, often associated with a zest for life. While traditionally used as a common noun, it has been embraced as a unique given name for both boys and girls.

Its origin traces back to English, where it directly stems from the word describing excitement and fervor. Some interpretations also link it to the French word zeste, which refers to the outer peel of citrus fruits, symbolizing vibrancy and flavor.
