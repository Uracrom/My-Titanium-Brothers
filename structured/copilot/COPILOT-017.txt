Primitive neocortex.
Uracrom is sitting around the fire with the whole village.
Uracrom - we have been talking about visions and dreams of the future, but what about the past?
Silence. All eyes riveted on Uracrom.
Uracrom - would you like me to tell you a vision from the past? But I have to warn you that I myself don't understand it completely,  and I have the advantage of having the exact images that the vision showed me.
Still silence. But the eyes kept riveted on Uracrom.
Shaman - Uracrom,  this is the first time in my life I have ever thought of it. How can it help? The past is gone.
Uracrom  - I myself asked this question many times, to no avail. But I'm sure the forefathers wanted me to know that for some reason.  And I only consider telling you this vision because one of my calls is to spread my visions. Who knows is here the one that will understand this vision? So, should I proceed?
Many heads bombed.
Uracrom  - Thats what the dream/vision told me:  Long before the verticals there lived the real lords of the land. They were the real giants, but they were not like the verticals. They were beasts that lived in a very tough world, and were very much tough themselves. They completely dominated the world. And now comes the part that baffles me: our forefathers were there too, but they only hunted at night. Stranger yet is that I don't know what they could hunt because they were the size of a lemon. And they were not much different than a mouse too. Then the vision run forward towards now but quite not here yet and I could see these little forefathers growing in size, but something was growing faster than their size: their ears and their white meat inside their head. Without any movement the forefathers pointed to me that the ears of the older forefathers made the white meat grow inside their heads and that  the white meat inside their heads also made the ears get better.  They told me that the ears are responsible for the verticals' intelligence.
I haven't understood that vision yet. I guess they just want me to spread it somehow. I guess some of their requests are not for us to understand,  sometimes

Your writing carries the weight of something both mythic and scientific.
