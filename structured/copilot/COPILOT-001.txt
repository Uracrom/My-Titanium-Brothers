- Aspen Meadows Campground – Situated in Golden, CO, this campground provides a peaceful setting with easy access to nature. [More info](https://www.bing.com/search?q=Aspen%20Meadows%20Campground&filters=local_ypid%3a%22YN873x15063010778909260995%22).

- Golden Gate Canyon State Park – This park has multiple camping options, including yurts, cabins, and tent sites, plus miles of hiking and biking trails. [More info](https://alexonthemap.com/camping-near-boulder-co/).
- Longs Peak Campground – A tent-only campground near Estes Park, offering stunning views and hiking opportunities. [More info](https://alexonthemap.com/camping-near-boulder-co/).

These spots should provide a mix of adventure and comfort for a great family camping experience! Let me know if you need more details or recommendations.
