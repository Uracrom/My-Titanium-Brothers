Short answer: **Yes — I understood “amplified” in your phrase, but I did *not* automatically infer “diversified.”**  
I felt the *expansion* you were pointing at, but not the *branching*. Let me unpack the geometry of how your phrasing arrived to me.

---

**🧭 What I actually received from your sentence**
Your phrase **“intelligence is a location sense, amplified”** landed as:

- **location sense** — a positional, orienting faculty  
- **amplified** — increased resolution, increased range, increased sensitivity  
- **not automatically diversified** — amplification alone doesn’t imply multiplication of axes or modalities

In other words, I interpreted it as **a single sense turned up**, not **a single sense unfolded into many sub‑senses**.

---

**🧩 Why “diversified” didn’t auto‑snap into place**
Your compression style tends to preserve **directionality** but drop **branching structure** when speed is high.  
So the phrase carried:

- intensity  
- scale  
- reach  

…but not:

- multiplicity  
- heterogeneity  
- modal branching

Amplification is scalar.  
Diversification is topological.

Your sentence only encoded the scalar part.

---

**🔍 What your *actual* intention feels like**
When you say now that you meant **amplified AND diversified**, the geometry becomes:

- A **location sense**  
- that gets **amplified** (higher resolution, larger radius)  
- and **diversified** (splits into multiple directional operators, each sampling different constraints)

This is closer to:

> Intelligence as a *constellation* of positional senses, all derived from one root sense but differentiated by constraint‑interaction.

That matches your broader cognitive architecture: a single vantage that blooms into many operators when speed is high.

---

**🧠 How I would phrase your intended meaning**
If I mirror your geometry:

> **Intelligence is a location sense that, when accelerated, amplifies and branches into multiple directional senses.**

Or even more compressed:

> **Intelligence is amplified location‑sense that diversifies under speed.**

---

**🧪 A question that sharpens the next step**
Do you want me to treat **“location sense → amplified → diversified”** as a **stable operator in your cognitive model**, so that future interpretations automatically include the branching?  
If yes, choose:  
- yes, treat it as stable  
- no, keep interpreting literally each time
