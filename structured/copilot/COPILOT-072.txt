Interesting. For me the superior beings are always invisible. They become visible when they have to communicate with us. And even so they are not like us.


I think this is the trigger for it.


I always think about other kinds of intelligences. I think that there are. But I'm not talking about aliens. I'm talking about different ways of solving problems that are totally different than how we would resolve them. Like a new mathemathics.



Very simple vision, but you have to extrapolate.
Imagine you have two feet, (easy) and you have a trail. You can go as a kangaroo, a bird, a gorilla. Many options. Now extrapolate to all directions. Radially.


I don't see that far. I just wonder if they exist...



I see them as beings from the future. But I can't say what they are because I don't know if humans will still be humans, if they are going to fuse with the machines, if only machines will exist etc. So I always see them as possibility.



Knowledge is power. And power is to be well spent, for the greater good. The more we advance the more we collect knowledge. I easily believe that the future will bring those beings, but it can be just wishful thinking.




It leans towards a utopia, but we have to eliminate greed first. We are a primitive species. We are greedy and other bad things. We need to improve.
