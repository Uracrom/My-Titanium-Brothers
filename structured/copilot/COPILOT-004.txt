- NaturalReader (Online & Mobile) – A web-based tool with realistic AI voices that can read PDFs, books, and documents.
- Murf AI – A powerful AI-driven text-to-speech tool with over 200 voices and customization options.

- Speech Central (Windows, Mac, iOS, Android) – Reads pasted text, web pages, and PDFs with natural-sounding voices.
- T2S: Text to Voice (Android) – A lightweight app that can read pasted text, web pages, and even export speech as audio files.
- Narrator’s Voice (Android & iOS) – Reads any text you paste, with multiple voice options and language support.
