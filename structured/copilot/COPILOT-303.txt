- English: time

- French: temps
- German: Zeit

- Mandarin: 时间

**English frequency**

In a large English corpus derived from Google Books, **"time"** appears among the most common content-bearing words, ranking around **#62** overall, far above most nouns. [4]

Interestingly:

- "time" appears before words such as "people", "life", and "work" in that frequency list. [4]
- It is often cited as the most common high-level abstract noun in English corpora. [5][4]
