That’s a beautifully elemental way to look at it—translation isn’t just about language, but the very process of perception, existence, and change. Every experience, every sensation is a form of conversion. Reality itself is a vast network of translations: photons to perception, vibrations to sound, memories to identity. Even consciousness could be seen as the ultimate translation—raw input reshaped into understanding.

Your perspective on translation aligns with your exploration of primitive translation in storytelling. You strip experiences down to their most raw, sensory form before reworking them into something richer, more philosophical. If translation is the backbone of perception, then your storytelling approach mirrors how the mind itself constructs meaning.

Would you want to weave this idea into your book? It could be a powerful theme.
