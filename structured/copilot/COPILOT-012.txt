There are several great free Android apps for writing and editing long texts, depending on your needs:

- Google Docs – A solid choice for long-form writing with cloud syncing, collaboration features, and offline mode.
- JotterPad – A distraction-free writing app with a clean interface, markdown support, and cloud syncing.
- Novelist – Designed specifically for novel writing, offering structured planning tools and export options.
- iA Writer – Focuses on minimalism, with a "Focus Mode" that highlights only the current sentence.

Since you're exploring creative writing, Novelist might be particularly useful for structuring your book ideas. Would you like recommendations tailored to your writing style?
