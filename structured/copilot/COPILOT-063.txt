I "updated the above text. Please tell me what you think.

Yesterday I was gifted with another time vision. I'm going to describe it first, then I'll explain it if I can't explain it on the fly while "drawing" it.
I saw something with a curvy cone shape, pointing up. I didn't see the bottom part of it, just the "tip". Its silhouette was just like a rifle bullet with the very tip blunt, not "pointy". It had a fine like silk kind of shroud covering it, and this shroud was softly pressed over the shape, like if the shape was moving up and the wind was pressing the shroud onto it, but not tight. The shroud was colorful but only blue, green, and shades of gray from almost black to almost white. Blue was predominant, then green then almost black, then gray then light gray. There were fine ragged lines and some dots too, like it was sprinkled with some liquid.
But the important thing was what was going "inside" "time" (it was, after all, an "image" of time). The meaning it stamped in my head the moment it showed up was that  the present IS the "part of time" or the "portion of time" that is actually farther ahead in time! The present is what hits the future (which, strangely but logically, isn't there) first. Or it doesn't hit anything. It just keeps advancing, leaving behind a trail that we call "past". But in this scenario, where is the future? I saw it as a "counter trail" or an anti trail" that moves itself "inside" the present and past trail, like it is contained or constrained by it. The vision "told me" that it is like that because the future can only happen based on the past and present. It cannot just be independent. It needs the present, aided by the past, to blaze a trail where the future can happen, even if it is just for the flimsy moment before it becomes the present, which also disappears as soon as it's formed, living space to the new, ever changing, present.

(Additional "view")

Now, a day or two later, what looked like an insignificant detail takes a literal leap in importance. In the above description I didn't mention from where I was observing that image exactly. My point of view was basically the same plane as the blunt tip of "time". The insignificant detail was the "tip", the front most part of the blunt front. There was a too small to see "point of interest" but I didn't pay any attention to it due to the gigantic "understanding" and novelty of the image. I "noticed" that "noise" in the image but there was much to say and it disappeared from the important things "shelf". But today I decided to magnify that spot and boom! There we were, standing on a big plane, so vast that it looked straight, not curved, just like we see the Earth: an "infinite" plain, not a sphere. Then I realized that this visual was fusing or understanding an old view of the present I had, where I saw the future as a membrane that advanced towards the future at "times speed", perpendicular to the direction of the future. An easy way to visualize this is imagining you approaching a wall head on: you are the future and the wall, the membrane. But this was in the past. Now I think differently.
