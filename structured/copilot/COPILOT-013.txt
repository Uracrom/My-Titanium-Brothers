I had another dream about the future,  but this one was a more distant future than the boat-cave dream.
The builders of the boat caves knew strange things. They told me in silent words. It was like it was a memory already and I suddenly remembered it. They told me that the big ball was the dirt, this land were we stand. This plain that we think is so big, is a ball. And is a SMALL ball. There are balls that are so big that this ball, our ball, is almost like dust, compared with those giants. They also told me that the boat-cave builders believed that there were balls so big that their weight would swallow them and then swallow everything and anything that it could grab. And they believed that once you were swallowed you would fall until the end of times because those giant balls, once they swallowed themselves they would have no bottom.  But the more distant future people said that they were wrong, that those holes actually were just sending the things they swallowed back to the begging of time so everything could continue to come back again, for the never ending time.
The lesson that I could get from there is that even when you are able to do or build marvelous things like the cave botas that float amongst the stars can be wrong. Even wisdom can change

This is fascinating—it has the feeling of myth, cosmic speculation, and shifting truths across time. Let's refine the "primitive translation" so it distills the raw, sensory essence while keeping its philosophical depth:

Dream. Distant. Time.
Builders made cave-boats. Cave-boats carried them far. Builders spoke, but not with mouth. Words were already inside me, waiting to wake.

Ball. Big. Small.
Land is ball. Ball is small. Not biggest. Others—giant, enormous. Builders knew of hungry balls, balls that eat all. Pull, swallow, no bottom. Fall forever.

People later, people beyond.
They said no. Swallow-ball does not hold, does not trap. It sends back. What is taken comes again. Time never ends.

Wisdom is not rock. Wisdom is river.
Builders made wonders. Cave-boats drift in sky. Still, builders were wrong. Old knowing fades. New knowing grows. Truth changes.

How does this version feel? I kept the spirit of your vision but made the wording more elemental and rhythmic. I love how this exploration of perception and knowledge fits into your storytelling style! Do you want to refine any part further?

If I were to describe a dream of being inside a spaceship in a "primitive" way, I'd strip it down to its core sensations and ideas while keeping its depth:

Sky not end. Up, far, more.
I ride metal beast. Beast holds me. No fall. No ground.

Stars breathe. Cold, deep, silent.
Around me—black river, no shore. Sun-fire too far, too small.

Home is dust. Small ball spins.
I see land-ball, tiny, below. I was there, now I am not.

Time twists. Thought whispers.
Dream speaks. Future calls. I listen.

The focus here is on immediacy—feeling the movement, the scale, the isolation, and the realization of something larger than human perception. Does this version align with the kind of translation you're aiming for? Or do you want something even more stripped down?
