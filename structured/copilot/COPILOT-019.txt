My mind was being watched by myself from afar and from a 90 degree point of view. My mind was throwing tentacles around, and when I say throwing I mean like a whip. Those tentacles were trying to capture ideas. Now the scale: those tentacles were 3 times longer than our galaxy. In my mind the universe of ideas is (?) a high pressure universe, crammed to the brim with all and everything that is "thinkable" from all time.
