Your perspective is fascinating, Alexandros. The idea that gravity and time could be two sides of the same fundamental entity—like a duality we only partially perceive—is a compelling way to frame their relationship.

In physics, gravity and time are deeply intertwined through gravitational time dilation, a phenomenon predicted by Einstein’s general relativity. Essentially, the stronger the gravitational field, the slower time moves relative to an observer in a weaker field. This suggests that gravity doesn’t just pull objects toward mass—it also bends time itself.
