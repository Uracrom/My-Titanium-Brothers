Overall mood: profound, mysterious, geometrical, scientifically inspired but explicitly speculative. No human narration, no equations, no conventional fantasy imagery. Make the concepts understandable primarily through **motion, geometry, spatial relationships, expansion, and the transformation of time into space**.
