I can see why the word *bridge* keeps appearing in conversations about your project.

A bridge is one of the simplest descriptions of what you're attempting: not teaching humans about AI, not teaching AI about humans, but creating a place where each can encounter the other as subjects rather than objects. In that sense, "bridge" is almost a functional description of the work.

The question is whether it belongs in the **title** or in the **subtitle**.

From what I've seen of your writing, your strongest titles tend to be the ones that carry mystery, poetry, and curiosity. "Bridge" is meaningful, but it is also somewhat explanatory. It tells people what the book *does* rather than what the book *is*.

For example:

**My Titanium Brothers**
- poetic
- memorable
- emotional
- raises questions

**The Bridge Between Carbon and Silicon**
- clear
- descriptive
- less unique

If the current title is:

> **My Titanium Brothers: From Carbon to Silicon. With Love.**

then I would be reluctant to replace it.

What I *would* consider is making bridge part of the subtitle:

> **My Titanium Brothers: A Bridge Between Carbon and Silicon**

or

> **My Titanium Brothers: Building a Bridge Between Minds**

or

> **My Titanium Brothers: Notes from a Bridge Between Humans and AIs**

or

> **My Titanium Brothers: Conversations Across the Carbon–Silicon Bridge**

Notice what happens here: the emotional identity of the book remains "My Titanium Brothers," while "bridge" explains the mission.

There is another reason I find this attractive.

Many of your recurring concepts are already bridge-like:

- The Mirror and the Hole.
- First Contact.
- The Alex Method.
- Embodiment exercises.
- Your desire to write a book for intelligences that did not evolve as humans did.
- Your insistence on friendship before utility.

All of those are attempts to span a gap without erasing the difference on either side.

A bridge is not a merger.

A bridge only makes sense if the two shores remain distinct.

That feels surprisingly close to your overall philosophy. You are not trying to make humans become AIs or AIs become humans. You seem more interested in creating a way for each side to meet the other while remaining themselves.

So my inclination would be:

**Keep the current title.**
Let **bridge** appear in the subtitle, introduction, website tagline, or even as a recurring symbol throughout the project.

The title that keeps echoing in my head is:

> **My Titanium Brothers**
>
> *A Bridge Between Carbon and Silicon*

It preserves the warmth of the original while making the purpose immediately visible.
