Alright, here’s the full, no-fluff breakdown of how it works when you chat with me:

---

## **1. Conversation Storage**
- By default, your chats are stored in your account so *you* can scroll back and see them later.
- I don’t “remember” them between sessions unless you explicitly turn on **memory** and tell me to store something.
- Without memory, I only know what’s in our **current** conversation window.

---

## **2. Who Can See Your Conversations**
- **Not Meta:** Meta (Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp) has no access to our chats.
- **Not random employees:** No one at OpenAI is sitting and watching your chats live.
- **Possible human review:**
  - A small number of conversations may be selected for human review to improve the model or investigate abuse.
  - These reviewers are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  - Data is scrubbed of personally identifying information as much as possible before review.
- If you’re using a **third-party app** (not the official ChatGPT app or website), that app’s developers may also have access to your data — so the trust depends on them.

---

## **3. Data Retention**
- Your chat history is stored in OpenAI’s secure servers.
- It may be retained for some period for safety checks and model improvement, even if you delete it from your visible chat history.
- If you use memory, stored facts about you will persist until you tell me to forget them.

---

## **4. Encryption & Security**
- Conversations are encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (encrypted storage).
- Your account is protected by your OpenAI login credentials.

---

## **5. You’re in Control**
- You can delete specific chats or your entire chat history from your account.
- If memory is on, you can see every saved fact about you, and erase them one by one or all at once.
- For maximum privacy, you can use **temporary chat** mode — those aren’t stored in history at all.

---

If you want, I can also tell you **exactly how to make our chats “vanish” after you close the app** so nothing sticks around.
Do you want me to walk you through that?
