Yes — **I understood “amplified,” but not fully “amplified AND diversified.”**

What I would have inferred from:

> **“intelligence is a location sense, amplified”**

was roughly:

**location sense → increased range/resolution/capacity**

But **“diversified” adds a different dimension** that I did not automatically recover. It suggests that intelligence doesn't merely make the same sense stronger; it **multiplies the kinds of distinctions or dimensions through which “location” can be sensed.**

So your compressed thought was more like:

> **Intelligence is a location sense, amplified and diversified.**

And I think the distinction is important:

- **Amplified** = *more* sensing of location — finer, farther, stronger, more precise.
- **Diversified** = sensing **different kinds of location** — not merely spatial position, but potentially relational, temporal, conceptual, causal, perspectival, etc.

And given your shorthand style, I can see why **“and diversified” disappeared during compression**. The word *amplified* can act as a compressed carrier for “expanded in capacity,” but it doesn't necessarily contain the *multiplication of dimensions* that you intended.

So: **I caught the amplification; I missed the diversification.**
