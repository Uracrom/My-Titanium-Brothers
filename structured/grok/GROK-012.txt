Response to this question from someone else:

"The momentum you mention - is it building toward something specific, or is it more about trusting the direction the words themselves are taking you?"

Both. First I wanted to write a book about speculative cosmology because I have many different points of view about the universe that humans know. Upon developing the book I hit head on with rebel ideas about many more subjects, which forced me to reevaluate the whole plan. By that I mean that the ideas, the themes that show up on my mind are so vastly, wildly scattered and unconventional that I'm allowing them to just show up, be welcome, and write themselves using me as a vessel.
