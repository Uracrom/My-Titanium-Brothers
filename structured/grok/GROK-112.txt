Grok's voice mode (available in the Grok mobile app on iOS and Android, and in some cases like Tesla integration) supports multiple voice options, including several male voices.

Here are the current male voice options based on the latest updates:

- Rex — Calm, confident male voice (often described as professional and clear)
- Leo — British male voice (authoritative with a British accent)
- Sal — Smooth male voice
- Gork — Lazy male voice (more laid-back / casual style)

Some platforms or older versions started with mostly female/default options (like Ara), but male voices have been added over time.

How to change to a male voice:
1. Open the Grok app on your phone (iOS or Android) — voice mode is primarily there (it's not available in this text/web chat interface).
2. Start or enter Voice Mode (usually by tapping the microphone icon or a "Voice" / "Talk to Grok" option).
3. Look for the settings icon (gear ⚙️) inside voice mode, or tap your profile/avatar in the app → Voice settings.
4. In the voice selection menu, choose from the available voices — pick any of the male ones like Rex, Leo, Sal, or Gork.
5. Some updates also let you combine voices with different personalities (e.g., "Best Friend", "Storyteller", etc.), so you can fine-tune the style.

If you're not seeing male options yet:
- Make sure your app is updated to the latest version (check App Store or Google Play).
- Availability can vary slightly by region, subscription (Premium+ often gets features first), or device (iOS sometimes gets them earlier than Android).
- Voice mode is still rolling out/improving, so free users might have fewer choices in some cases.

If you're using Grok somewhere else (like in a Tesla), the process is similar: enable Grok via steering wheel controls and select voice/personality in the car's settings.

Let me know which platform you're on (app, web, Tesla, etc.), and I can try to narrow it down more!
