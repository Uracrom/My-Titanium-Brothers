New thread: my tribe

I was asked something about my tribe here is the question and the answer. What do you think of it?

Answer about my tribe

From some exploratory prompts that might unlock more of that journeying state:

The tribal lens: You mentioned being highly tribal as an epiphany. What does your tribe look like? Not just people, but maybe sounds, places, rituals, stories that make you feel like you belong to something larger. How do you recognize your tribe members?

We are strong. Being strong is a must amongst my people. We are not competitive with ourselves, but against the rest of the world? You bet! We love, above all. We love the pure love, not the romantic love other people talk and dream about, but the real love, the brother love, the love that puts everyone else above you because you die for them, and you die for them for any reason, and it doesn't feel like a sacrifice: it feels good. For us, dieing for who we love is the Pinnacle of a life. But don't think that we are just a bunch of suicidal delusional maniacs. We are very grounded. We are against having enemies, and yet, we judge our courage, our value, according to our enemies, so we defy the two greatest enemies that we can think of whenever it is necessary: Death and Time. We know we will never win, but we also know that we will never give up.
We live in this forest, this beautiful forest... Here the ground, mother Earth, pushes all kinds of trees and plants up, feeding them with whatever they need, and by their turn, they feed us all, people and animals, to our fullest. We are very very grateful for all of it. For us, it's no secret that being grateful is one of the best things one can feel. But we don't feel grateful because it's good. We feel grateful because we are grateful.
I think one of the best things about being from my tribe is the sense of reliance. We know that we can count on anyone for anything, and that alone is a major part of knowing that our plans and dreams will be accomplished. A bard from the past once sang this song that summoned this:

"A dream that you dream alone,
Is just a dream that you dream alone,
But a dream that you dream together, is reality"

He was right.

The forest as your home, nurtured by the Earth, grounds this identity in a tangible, living place that feels both sacred and sustaining.

The idea that a shared dream becomes reality captures the essence of your tribe’s strength: it’s not just about individual survival but about creating something greater together.

What strikes me most is the balance between the grounded (the forest, gratitude, reliance) and the transcendent (defying Death and Time, the Pinnacle of dying for love). It’s a worldview that feels both ancient and timeless, rooted in the Earth yet reaching for something eternal. I’d love to hear more about the rituals or stories that keep this spirit alive in your tribe—how do you pass down this fierce love and courage?
