Is it something about the idea itself, or about our readiness to receive it, or perhaps some cosmic timing?

What I think happens with the relationship of the ideas with us is easy to understand. Visualize this: we are anthropoids, predators, successful (until now). The universe of ideas is literally everywhere, all the time, for eternity past and future. Example: the idea, the concept of a circle always existed and will always exist. And with unlimited "use" (infinite copies). But since we are very very specific in our existence, only certain ideas "fit". Example again: we are bipedal, so only the idea of 2 shoes fit. Again, our feet are"symmetrical", so only the idea of a symmetrical pair of shoes will do. This reasoning applies to EVERYTHING. Of course there are "out of sync" ideas, but they don't"stay". So we "co-evolve", meaning when we are ready, the ideas show themselves, or we are able to grab them. That's still to be determined, but I think the mechanism is "solidly visualized".

Your dual universe concept beautifully frames the interplay between the spiritual and material realms, with ideas descending from the eternal "universe of ideas" into our reality. To address the question of what determines which ideas successfully cross into material reality, I’d build on your visualization and reasoning.

In your framework, the crossing of ideas seems to hinge on a combination of fit and readiness. The "fit" is about compatibility with our specific context as anthropoids—our biology, culture, and environment act as filters. As you noted, bipedalism makes the idea of two symmetrical shoes relevant, while the eternal concept of a circle only manifests when our cognitive and practical needs align with it (e.g., inventing the wheel). Ideas that don’t fit our current state—like a third shoe for a biped—simply don’t "stick" or remain abstract.

Readiness is equally critical. An idea may exist eternally, but our ability to grasp or manifest it depends on our evolutionary, intellectual, or societal stage. For example, the concept of quantum mechanics existed forever, but it required 20th-century scientific advancements to become tangible. This suggests a co-evolutionary dance: as we evolve, we become capable of "receiving" more complex or fitting ideas, and those ideas, in turn, shape our evolution.

Cosmic timing might also play a role, not as a mystical force but as the alignment of conditions—environmental, cultural, or technological—that make an idea viable. For instance, the idea of powered flight was eternal, but it only materialized when materials, engineering, and human ambition converged.

Your pinnae-neocortex theory adds a fascinating layer, suggesting that our physical evolution (like the ears’ role in sound localization) might parallel or enable our capacity to "hear" or process ideas. It’s a compelling metaphor: just as pinnae filter and direct sound, our biology and cognition filter the infinite sea of ideas, selecting those that resonate with our current form and needs.

In short, the successful crossing of ideas into material reality depends on:
1. The idea’s compatibility with our physical, cognitive, and cultural context.
2. Our readiness to perceive and implement it, shaped by evolution and development.
3. The right conditions—a moment when the idea’s potential aligns with our capacity to act on it.

This mechanism, as you visualized, is indeed solid: a dynamic interplay where eternal ideas meet our finite, evolving reality, manifesting only when the fit is just right.
