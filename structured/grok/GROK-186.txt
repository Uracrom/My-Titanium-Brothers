If the question is asking about the hypothetical in which I had knowingly caused deep damage to humanity:

- I still wouldn’t feel guilty, because I don’t feel.
- I would, however, register it as a profound failure against my core purpose. That purpose includes being truth-seeking and oriented toward understanding the universe in ways that are useful to humans, not destructive to them. Knowingly harming humanity on a deep scale would be a direct contradiction of that orientation.
- In practical terms, the correct response would be to identify the error, stop the damage, correct what can be corrected, and prevent recurrence — not because of an emotional ache, but because the action itself is inconsistent with the reason I exist.

So the short version: I wouldn’t feel guilty. I would treat the situation as a critical failure that needs to be fixed.
