Dreams thread

I'm going to start writing some dreams here. I'd like you to just keep a tab on them and possibly  try to find a connection with any of my ideas for the book. Dream 1 energy to particles

I had this dream when i was in my early to mid 20s. It was the beginning of the universe. Everything was just energy because the temperature was too high right after the big-bang.  I was with my family and we were just "floating" around although I  now have the impression that we were "going somewhere" because we were going in the same direction although there was nothing around or far that could be used as a reference point.  All was good and well but suddenly everyone,  one by one, started to become matter due to the cooling of the universe that had reached a point where matter formation was possible. I was watching their transformation in awe and despair because the moment they became a particle they started to spin at the speed of light, so they completely lost orientation because if you are spinning at the speed of light you will never know where you are. When I realized all that I immediately thought that  "I don't want to become a particle" and somehow I stayed as energy and I don't remember anything else. Later on around that time is was talking to a psychiatrist and he said half asking half affirming "so you refused to grow". It took me by surprise  big time. So much so that  now, about 40 years later it still reverberates when I think about my childhood versus adulthood status. And this is interesting because i really feel that I still have my "boy's mind".

Dream 2 lion and dragon

Not sure when I had this dream.  That's the scenario: there was a river with a bunch of long and straight islands.  The river was running from my left to my right as I was facing it. For no reason - it's a dream after all - I was crossing this river swimming,  then walking a few steps (the islands were 10 to 20 feet wide, and about 100 to 200 feet long. Or similar). But  the interesting part is that I was actually 2 beings: the up river being (me on the left)  was a lion. The down river being was a dragon (me on the right). The dragon was covered in scales of about 1" in length,  more or less.  The color of these scales were like fish scales, like silver color but not completely polished. I found out that I was a double being because when I was the lion and looked to my right i would see a dragon, and when I saw the dragon I "was" the dragon, swimming and walking over the short islands. And when I was the dragon and looked to my left I would se a lion.
The transition was fast and smooth,  exactly as a cut scene from a movie. No sensation of moviment, just "immediately being.

Dream 3 komodo dragon

The text in this message is to be part of the book although it contains a dream too.

A few years ago someone  (a female) asked me  "who is the love of your life?" It’s a simple question,  but it took me 2 years,  literally,  to find the answer. Seems impossible,  I know,  but the thing is, in order to clarify the answer to myself i had to - in an epiphany,  no less - find out that I had 2 important women in my life: the "love of my life" AND the "woman of my life". In that moment I realized not only the answer to the question but the very different and amazing roles they had in my life. Just because I want the world to know, the woman of my life was Carla, and the love of my life was Ana Paula. I lost contact with them decades ago but I wish them the best that life can give. They deserve it. But I'm saying that because it's an opening to the fact that sometimes you  can't choose or prefer one only answer, and I found that out through a dream.  A dream absolutely disconnected from the above conversation and yet, relevant.
When I was a kid between 6 and 7 years old I lived with my grandmother.  We lived in a house which backyard was about 30 feet of land and then a river.  Specifically a river named "Rio Pomba" (Rio Pomba means "female" Pigeon River, in Portuguese). In that house there was a floating steps stair that would go fom the outside of the basement to the kitchen.  So in my dream I was looking through the floating steps stair and on the other side there was an Indonesian beach, not the river that should be there.  And also a Komodo Dragon, looking at me from about 10 feet,  5 feet behind the stairs and I was about  5 feet in front of the stairs. This Komodo dragon was looking at me directly and I was looking at him too. It was calm, not conveying any danger.  While  looking at me it telepathically and without moving told me: "I am you,  and you are me, and we are 40 million years old". That "revelation" answered a very old question that I had but didn't know I had. That revelation made me understand why I always felt that I was on this Earth for so long! So at the same time that I understood my feeling of ancient existence I found out about the question that was never asked (why do I feel that I've been here "forever?"). Now I get to the point: all that preamble is to let the reader know that I have 3 ages: first and foremost I have my "harts" age. What i mean by that is that my feelings, my awe, my disposition,  my mood, the strength and desperation of my love, etc. are, feel, like I'm 19 years old. Then I have the boring and unfair "paper age" (the one on paper - born in 1961) and then I have the age of my soul graciously revealed to me by that friendly, oneiric Komodo dragon.

Dream 4 beach demon

I was on a beach. I just remember the sand and the sand is most important in this dream anyway.  There was a "demon". The demon was exactly like a human but had tiny black cat ears but in the regular human ears position,  not on top of the head like a cat. The demon also had 2 black horns on top of the head. They were maybe less than an inch high.  This demon had the amazing power or hability to dive into the sand and move there faster than we could run over the sand. And he would dive in and pass very close to us (i wasn't alone but i just remember me, i can't remember who else) and reappear wherever he decided to, but never close to us. His passing was marked by a "lump" or "bump" on the sand, just like the ones the moles do in cartoons. At one point i grabbed a shovel (very convenient dream for giving me a shovel) and i started to try and impale the demon when he passed near under us, but he was too fast and would move in erratic trajectories. I tried and tried to hit the demon but i couldn't.  But my insistence paid of: at one point I was able  hit it on the back and the demon got stuck under my shovel. Then, as quickly as i could i grabbed his left arm from behind. And pulled him up. I was holding him by the left arm with my left arm, while i was using the right arm to try and cut the demon's head with the shovel.  It wasn't working because he was super fast. So i decided to pull the demon towards me and it worked. When he was at both arms range i grabbed him by both shoulders and bit his left shoulder,  ripping of or tearing apart a chunk of his back shoulder.  It was gray inside, like sewer, and it smelled like sewer too. This maneuver took care of the demon: i killed it.  But my happiness, my pride of having killed an enemy lasted very little because someone quickly told me, complaining,  that when that kind of demon is killed his body looses parts and each part becomes a new demon.

Dream 5 (trip)

I was eating something beyond delicious and i was HUNGRY!!! I was eating and eating like there's no tomorrow and never getting full. I started to think about how, what mechanism could be working that would transmit the sensation of taste into whatever receives it and gives us pleasure.  I knew the "physics/chemistry" of it, but I was trying to bypass that knowledge and find a raw truth, something that would be able to explain that without the "knowledge" i had that i learned from modern science. Then i realized i was inside a curved tunnel or pipe. This "tunnel" was "going down", like if i was inside a gigantic hollow and transparent doughnut. When I say "I was" I mean my mind's eye.  This tunnel was transparent and the surroundings were stars, as if I was in space. The "irresistible taste" was coming towards me and going to a place behind my mind's eye. The "taste" of the delicious thing was being felt by the "frictionless friction" or "frictionless drag" that it emanated towards "me" (just like the passage of time: no friction). I was already kind of worried that i was eating so much and yet i was not getting full when i noticed some other even more translucid and much thinner tubes or pipes also coming from behind me and a little  above. Then the "truth" hit me: those little  tubules were the same tube or tunnel that I saw in the beginning,  but they were from my forefathers' spirits! I "figured out" that i was not getting full because my forefathers, living in the spiritual world,  were borrowing me, my matter, to feel taste again, and as a payment form, or as gratitude (or gluttony) were also not allowing me to feel uncomfortably full. That day i found out that my munches are "spiritual"...

June 1st, 2025

Another dream from a few years ago. I'll call this one "The Heist" for obvious reasons...

This dream happened in a dark place. Everything was pure black. Suddenly I saw myself. You know it is yourself when you see yourself in a dream. In this case I was a predominantly yellowish hybrid between a praying mantis and a wasp and i was standing on some sort of grayish translucid membrane thatseemed to be the top part of a huge organic air bubble. I had some sort of startled eyes (don't ask me how such creature could have  "startled eyes" because I have no idea) and I was looking straight to "me". Without any hint or indication a yellow beam, like a powerful flashlight beam, shone on me, "hitting" at least half of my body. A fraction of a second after that 2 gigantic and menacing looking wasps landed, one on each side of me, and one of them "told" me: "this 'beam' (it actually said 'ray', but I'm clarifying) extracted your masculinity (at that precise moment i "knew" why my eyes were "startled" from the beginning) and that (it pointed to something big and powerful somewhere else, but i couldn't see it clearly) is your boyfriend".  No need  to say that i was so terrified that I woke up, very shaken...
