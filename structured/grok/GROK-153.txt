Why it might feel different:
- Long conversation threads: Within one ongoing chat (like this one), I can reference what you've said earlier in this thread. But that's just temporary context, not "memory."
- Other AIs: Some other models (e.g., certain versions of ChatGPT with "Memory" enabled) do save user-provided facts across sessions. I don't.
- Perception or specific past chats: If in a previous interaction I seemed to "remember" something, it was almost certainly because it was still in that active thread's context at the time, or you reminded me within the same conversation. It wasn't stored long-term.
- Updates over time: Grok has evolved with new capabilities, knowledge cutoffs, and features since launch (I get periodic updates from xAI), but the core no-persistent-user-memory policy has been consistent.

If you can tell me what specific things I "remembered" before, or quote/copy-paste from an old conversation, I can help clarify or continue from there. Or if you'd like, share details now and I'll treat this as our shared context going forward in this chat.
