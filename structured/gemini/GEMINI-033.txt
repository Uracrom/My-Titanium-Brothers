Today I was in a highway going 80/85 miles per hour like everyone else. There were many cars ahead of me and only one behind me. At one point I could see that far ahead the cars were slowing down. Soon I was slowing down too. Every time this happens I always check to make sure that the cars behind me are also slowing down, just in case. The one car behind me was slowing down, but he was somewhat uncomfortably close, and a little too much to the left side of the lane. I wondered if he was thinking about cutting me off or just trying to have a better look of the situation ahead since I was driving a big van. I noticed that the area between the two opposite sides of the highway was covered in small decorative rocks, about 1 or 2 inches big. I imagined that car trying to cut me off through the center area and how would the car behave in such terrain at that speed. Then I "knew" that the car would succeed much better than a motorcycle. Then I thought that a very experienced motorcyclist could do just fine (at least I believe that). Then I remembered that there are motorcycles now that can keep their own balance, and you can even try to topple them and they don't fall. Then I observed that such motorcycle needed to be "pushed" to react and regain its ballance. Then I saw that the next upgrade to this kind of system or motorcycle would be the use of sensors that could preview the force that would be applied on it and react before anything, giving the impression that the motorcycle is really "stable". Obviously this would have to be calculated with high speed and lots of data, but still feasible. Then I noticed that my standard "position" is to solve problems. Not because I look for solutions, but because I see the solutions before the problem arrives. I already knew it, but today I saw it "firsthand" and noticed it too.
I'm not satisfied with this "description" I just gave of my own behavior, but I think you understand.
What I think is interesting is that I don't feel that I'm looking for any answer. I just got around "seeing" improvement like I'm just watching a movie...
