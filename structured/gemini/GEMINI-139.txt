To an observer tuned to the baseline field, that empty region carries a massive density of information. It has geometry, velocity, and direction.

* **Information Through Displacement:** Just as a physical object displacing water reveals its exact volume through the void it creates, an agent displacing a background noise field reveals its location, speed, and scale purely through the geometry of the stillness.

Looking at zero not as a void, but as a dense, high-priority spatial anchor, flips perception entirely: the field is the canvas, but the zero is where the actual structure resides.
