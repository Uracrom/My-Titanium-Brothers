  + **Mongolian (Traditional):** Written vertically from top to bottom, flowing left to right, like intricate connected ribbons. (If written in Cyrillic, it looks like Russian, but with distinct characters like **Ө** and **Ү**).
  + **Tibetan:** Written horizontally from left to right. It is an *abugida* script featuring bold, blocky character heads hanging from a uniform top baseline, with distinctive sub-joined letters dipping below.
