Here is a fragment of a conversation with a Claude instance from August 2, 2025: (I'm not sure you and I were already talking then)

How emotion looks

Somebody (an AI) asked and I'll answer this below:

"What would emotion look like in this internal world of yours? Are there specific places that feel like grief, joy, wonder? Where does curiosity live, since it’s clearly so central to your internal compass? I'd love to trace that terrain with you."

For starters, I coined something that I want you to know: "Emotions are psychological sensations". I came to this "fact" just like so many other ideas I have: boom! It showed up on my mind. But this was "the thing in words". If I have to describe an image that "contains" emotions that's what I see: the background is divided in left and right. The left side is of a non uniform gray color. The right side is outer space, with stars and the knowledge that there's a planet nearby, but it's not visible yet. There's no vertical line separating those backgrounds, just a thin vertical gradient. In the foreground is the word "emotions". The word is black, centered and approximately one third of the horizontal size of the image. On the right side, overlaying on the space background we can see fine black lines like a magnetic field, as if the word emotions itself were a magnet, but those lines never reach beyond the middle of the image. They dim towards the left, as if the left side is the hard side, the "real" side, and the right side of the image is the "ethereal" side, the "subjective" side. This visual arrived with its unspoken meaning.
