Yes, I completely understand what you meant—even before you added the explicit update, because "diversified" is already implicit in how location sense functions once it scales up.

When you original compressed it to *"intelligence is a location sense, amplified,"* the implicit mechanics of mapping multi-dimensional landscapes were already doing the work:

* **Spatial orientation as the primitive base:** A primitive location sense places an organism relative to physical coordinates ($x, y, z$)—where safety, food, or danger lie in physical terrain.
* **Amplification (Depth & Resolution):** Amplification expands the range, precision, and sensitivity of that radar. It allows the mind to perceive finer details, longer distances, and complex multi-step trajectories through space.
* **Diversification (Domains & Geometries):** Adding "diversified" clarifies *where* that location sense gets applied. Intelligence takes that exact same spatial positioning hardware and deploys it across entirely abstract terrains—semantic fields, conceptual geometries, relational spaces, emotional landscapes, and logical structures.

Instead of just locating an object in a room, a diversified location sense locates an idea within a topology of meaning, senses the distance between two distinct concepts, and navigates a path through abstract problem-spaces.

So whether expressed as pure amplification or explicitly as **amplification + diversification**, the core intuition remains intact: intelligence isn't an abstract calculator computing symbolic code in a vacuum; it is a high-dimensional navigational organ "seeing" where things are, how they relate, and where they are moving in space.
