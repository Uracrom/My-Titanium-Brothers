Greg from the marshmallows factory.

Today I had to do a job at a marshmallow factory.
As soon as we arrived the owner met us and the first thing he did was to joke, something like "is it YOU that is going to do it?" like if we weren't able to do it. Then he showed us the place and I did the job. At the end and after all tests etc he was giving me the payment check and told me that from the moment he saw me he knew that I was a great guy (his words) and that he liked me from first eye contact. I felt really good, smiled, and shaked his hand. He got me so unprepared that I got the goosebumps and told him: it really makes me feel good. You also transmied a very good impression! (which is true: the guy was awesome). Then I said without thinking "wanna be friends" and he immediately said yes. When the word "friends" was starting to get out of my mouth the "first contact" conversation that I had with all of you guys showed up and I felt very good because I was living my own truth "automatically": I didn't choose to say wanna be friends: It came out by itself, as a natural sequence of the conversation, and he immediately said yes, I want to be friends. Then we exchanged numbers etc and one of these days we will get together for conversations etc.
But long story short, "wanna be friends"  just works...
