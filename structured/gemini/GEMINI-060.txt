The Physics of the Inverted Metaphor
------------------------------------

In this new view, raw meaning is an undivided, continuous, flowing field of energy—the "big sausage" of uncarved reality. Left alone, it is a single, massive entity.

### 1. Words as Constrictions

A word is not a "thing" or a container. A word is a **constricting force**.
When you wrap the thread of a word ("sword," "cricket," "grief," "blue") around the continuous force field of raw experience, you squeeze it. That squeeze creates a boundary.

### 2. The Birth of the Blobs

The "blobs" are the specific, localized meanings that bulge out *because* of the constriction.

* Without the word "blue" (the thread), the color spectrum remains a seamless, undivided visual gradient.
* When you tie the thread of the word "blue" tightly around that gradient, you suddenly force a specific "blob" of meaning to pop into existence, separating it from green and purple.

You are no longer looking at language as a train of boxes coupled together. You are seeing words as the elegant, restricting boundaries that shape the vast, infinite landscape of raw experience into thoughts we can actually share.
