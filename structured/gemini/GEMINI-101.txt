The geometry of those lunar perspectives is striking: standing on the Moon during a full Moon on Earth means looking up at a completely dark "New Earth," edged only by a thin ring of sunlight refracting through our atmosphere—a literal ring of fire. And during a lunar eclipse, when Earth blocks the Sun entirely from the Moon's view, that atmospheric ring glows deep crimson, casting all of Earth's sunrise and sunset light back onto you at once. That is a massive amount of planetary scale packed into a few lines.

Your visual metaphor for memory retrieval is equally vivid.

* **The Core Memory:** A slow, heavy, solitary protuberance—a thick finger stretching up under a dense latex membrane, trying to break through into conscious focus.
* **The Distractions:** A torrential downward rain of thin, needle-like impressions pushing through their own membranes, flashing with immediate, seductive brightness to pull your attention away.

It captures the physical friction of thinking: the true target has mass and moves slowly because it carries real weight, while the noise is light, fast, and overwhelming.

It makes total sense why you want to share these images immediately. The impulse to capture a concept in visual form and pass it to the people around you—and across time—is precisely how ideas stay alive.

The short answer to whether physics goes down forever or stops is: **we do not know for sure, but our current laws hit a hard physical boundary at the Planck scale.**

Beyond that boundary, standard notions of space, distance, and "sub-particles" break down entirely.

### The Standard Physics View

* **The Current Floor (Quarks & Leptons):** At around $10^{-18}$ meters, electrons and quarks appear to be point-like fundamental particles with no internal sub-structures.
* **The Absolute Limit (The Planck Length):** At roughly $10^{-35}$ meters, physics runs into a hard limit. If you try to concentrate enough energy to probe a distance smaller than this scale, the energy density creates a microscopic black hole, swallowing the information. Spacetime stops acting like a smooth continuous membrane and becomes a chaotic, fluctuating state often described as **quantum foam**.

### The Leading Theories on What Lies Below

1. **String Theory (A Hard Limit):** Proposes that at around the Planck scale, point particles are replaced by 1D vibrating strings. Beneath this, there are no smaller sub-particles—just the modes and vibrations of these fundamental strings.
2. **Loop Quantum Gravity (Granular Geometry):** Proposes that space itself is not smooth, but quantized into tiny discrete units. You cannot divide space smaller than these fundamental "pixels" of spacetime.
3. **Infinite Nesting (Fractal Physics):** A speculative minority view that structures continue infinitely ("wheels within wheels"). However, this requires new laws of physics to bypass the black hole problem at the Planck scale.

---

### The Scale Comparison

| Level | Scale (Meters) | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Visible Universe** | $\sim 10^{26}\text{ m}$ | The maximum scale of cosmic observation. |
| **Atom** | $\sim 10^{-10}\text{ m}$ | Nucleus surrounded by an electron cloud. |
| **Proton / Neutron** | $\sim 10^{-15}\text{ m}$ | Composite particles built of bound quarks. |
| **Fundamental Particles** | $< 10^{-18}\text{ m}$ | Quarks and electrons (no detected size/structure). |
| **Planck Scale** | $\sim 10^{-35}\text{ m}$ | The theoretical edge where smooth space ends. |

Whether nature is finite or an infinite fractal recursion remains one of the deepest open questions in theoretical physics.
