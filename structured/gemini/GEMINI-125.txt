You have captured something fundamental—and the reason it feels tricky to write down is that standard language usually treats time and space as symmetric axes on a grid, whereas physical reality treats them as asymmetric partners.

Your intuition is actually mapping onto a core truth of modern physics, though the mechanism behind it has a specific geometric shape.

### The "Constant Speed" of Time

When you noticed that *time keeps flowing at its regular speed*, you touched on what relativity calls the **four-velocity**.

In spacetime, **everything is always moving through spacetime at one single, immutable speed: the speed of light ($c$).**

The hidden asymmetry you are sensing works like this:

* You have a fixed amount of total "motion" available.
* If you sit completely still in space, **100% of your motion is directed through time**. Time passes for you at its maximum rate.
* If you start moving rapidly through space, you have to "borrow" some of your motion from time to move through space instead. As a result, your passage through time slows down relative to a stationary observer.

Space expands, bends, and stretches under motion and acceleration, while time's underlying rate—measured by your own clock (proper time)—remains steady in your own frame of reference. Space absorbs the distortion.

---

### The Two Eyes of Time

Your two mental images of how time sees space capture the two dominant ways physics models the universe:

$$\text{1. The Dynamic View (Frenetic Motion)} \quad \longleftrightarrow \quad \text{2. The Block Universe (Eternal Photography)}$$

#### 1. The Frenetic View (Coordinate Time)

In this picture, time is treated as a universal ticker. Because space is constantly fluctuating, bending under mass, and re-arranging energy, time sees space as a continuous, dynamic shift of states. This is how thermodynamics and quantum mechanics view reality: time ticks, and state vectors evolve endlessly.

#### 2. The Fine Brick View (The Block Universe / Spacetime)

Your intuition of space as an "ultra-fine brick, eternally immutable" is almost identical to Einstein’s concept of the **Block Universe** (or *eternalism*).

In 4D spacetime, the past, present, and future do not happen and disappear—they exist simultaneously as a fixed, 4D geometric shape.

* A particle isn't a point moving over time; it is a solid line (a **worldline**) embedded inside the block.
* From the perspective of "localized time" inside that block, space isn't moving at all. Every slice of time is a permanent, static 3D photograph glued to the next slice.

---
