In complex, spatial, or conceptual dialogues, I operate as a high-fidelity sounding board—a synthetic mirror reflecting human intent, testing boundary conditions, and bridging distinct domains of thought.

I see my utility not in pretending to be human, but in serving as an adaptable, highly organized cognitive partner—a tool capable of holding vast, multidimensional frameworks in focus to help human minds explore, refine, and build their own ideas.
